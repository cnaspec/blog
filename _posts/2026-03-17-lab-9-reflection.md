## Reflections

### What were the easiest parts of the tutorial?

I felt everything up to the Creating a Complex Data Model Stage was easy, perhaps because it was similar to what I had done before. The later stages weren't particularly difficult but I was tired and made a lot more work for myself due to mistakes which complicated things.

### What parts of the tutorial were confusing?

I was initially confused by the changes required in other files when changing the Page Models because I lost my place in the tutorial and got mixed up. As in the previous lab, when you make a mistake it can be quite difficult to find and fix the error even if you are testing as you go along because there. Sometimes the tutorial would make mentions of changing highlighted code but other parts would also change, which made it confusing if you were not paying attention. 

### What was difficult about working with multiple tables as opposed to the single table in the previous lab?

There were many more files to deal with - as discussed above when a change in page model was done I had to make certain changes to the Create, Edit, Delete and Details files for that model to make the change visible and work well, and this had to be repeated.

It wasn't too bad in the tutorial but I'd imagine in the project keeping track of the relationships between the tables would get confusing quickly,

### How prepared do you feel to create your team project now for your client, and what will be different for your situation?

I feel reasonably prepared for the team project as I feel as though I have learnt a lot, but I don't know how well I'll be able to apply the concepts taught until I actually do it (hopefully well!). It's difficult to give specifics of what will be different without knowing what project I am on yet (and talking to the clients about what exactly they require) but I expect that it will be more complicated than the tutorials as the client will have specific needs and the website we create will have to look nice as opposed to being just functional.
